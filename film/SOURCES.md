## Recorded, staged, and what the clips are not

- **Play clips** (`screens/clips/open`, `cast`): the game's own simulation and renderer, stepped one frame at a time (`requestAnimationFrame` off, `tick(t)` called with t advancing 1/30 s). A virtual clock runs the page during a clip: `performance.now()`, `setTimeout` and CSS animations follow t, so the kill banner, the headshot X, the kill flash and the finisher play at their own speed. The buy phase was ended with `world.roundTime = 0.01`, the pause the game shows when it has no pointer lock was hidden, and network toasts from the test API were hidden. The renderer is software (SwiftShader) at the game's “비주얼” preset: lower than a real GPU. No clip was edited.
- **How the opening's duel was set up** (all in `scripts/capture-clips.js`, logged in `clips.json` → `open.staged`): the round starts at one of glacier's spawn points (spawns are random each round); bots are frozen (`ADMIN.freezeAI`: they turn and shoot but do not walk) and the player takes no damage (`ADMIN.god`, no on-screen sign); two enemies are placed about 10 m ahead with heavy armour (50); the other three enemies are killed by teammates' bots before the clip (`dealDamage` credited to a teammate) and 8 s pass so the kill feed clears, which makes the second kill the round's last; teammates stand behind the camera; during the clip only the player's shots hurt the two enemies. The player's aim is a script that lands on the head-sphere centre; the spread is the game's own random spread, and the log shows what happened: 4 shots, 4 head hits, kills at frames 40 and 56 (5.7 m and 5.8 m), the phoenix finisher at frame 56.
- **Skins**: the film's account `hana` was signed up on the in-memory test API and given every skin with the owner page's own grant (`a_user_set`, op `grant`, item `all`), then equipped with the game's `GUN_SKINS.set` (opening: 봉황 on the rifle; skill clip: 심연). Production was not touched and no real account was used.
- **Map orbits** (`clips/map-*`): the home screen's own camera, UI hidden, advanced 0.2 s per frame.
- **Path screens** (`01…06`): the four clicks were real; after 전투 투입 the shop was closed and the buy timer set to 0.01 s so the game's `step()` opened the round (bots frozen so the view stayed still). The id typed was `hana`; the harness made the account.
- **Content screens** (`11-abilities`, `13-skins`): the menu, nothing staged.
- Not used: `07-planted.png`, `13-skins.png` (replaced by the five armoury shots), the phone screens, the earlier download-bar diagram.
