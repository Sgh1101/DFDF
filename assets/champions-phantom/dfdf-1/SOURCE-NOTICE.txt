Champions Phantom assets for DFDF (13).

Every file in this folder is original DFDF work, generated by code in this
repository: scripts/champions-phantom/ (npm run build:champions-phantom).
The gun uses DFDF bevelled solids and round bores; the dragon and serpent
are cube models built in code. Textures,
banner art and effect sprites are drawn by the same scripts; the kill,
inspect and finisher music is DFDF's own arrangement, played on instruments
that js/phantom-audio.js synthesizes in the browser. No third-party models,
textures, recordings or arrangements are included.
